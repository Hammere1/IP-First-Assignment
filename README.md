# Hammere Shimelse — Portfolio

A personal portfolio website introducing Hammere Shimelse, a Software Engineering student at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University. It highlights my skills, education, projects, design experience, and contact information.

## Pages

- **`index.html`** — Main portfolio page with an introduction, skills, featured projects, education, and contact details.
- **`projects.html`** — A fuller overview of projects and experience.
- **`thank you.html`** 
## Featured Work

- **Digital Cafeteria System** — A C++ and MySQL project focused on cafeteria records and operations.
- **Ethio-MicroLend Digital Loan Management System** — A C++ and MySQL project focused on microfinance and loan management.
- **Digital PC Management System** — A team project for managing computer information and usage records.
- **AI and Python projects** — Exploration of practical, data-driven solutions.

The portfolio also describes my internship experience at Zigzag Space Architectural Firm and my graphic design training through ALX.
